VO Pause Formatter

Paste a voice-over script, pick the engine that will read it, and copy back text with even, natural pauses.

How to use it

  1. Choose the engine that will read the script.
  2. Paste your script into the Script box, or load the sample.
  3. Pick a punctuation strength. Everything the tool adds is highlighted so you can check it.
  4. Copy the output into Azure, ElevenLabs or Storyline, and listen to one slide first.
